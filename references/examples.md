She (S1) answers softly with restrained delight, <d>[Chinese] 真的？你回来了？</d> She closes her lips and listens, keeping the phone at her right ear. Her eyes brighten without an exaggerated head movement. Preserve the reference identity, hairstyle and cardigan throughout. End on her listening smile, with her left hand resting on the cushion.

## 示例 C：拉帘后的情绪近景（连续剧情，计划 7 秒）
首帧承接 B；0–4 秒左手拉帘，4 秒切近景，4–7 秒反应。以下是独立片段，C01 本片仍是首位发声者 S1。
