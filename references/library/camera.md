# 运镜、视角与光学：48 项可复用方法

来源：U20261106-01 第三部分；下列英语均为本技能规范化改写，不是官方固定触发词，也不是已实测模型能力。相机、演员、地点和造型均按当前需求替换。花括号槽位在交付前补齐或删去；不机械复制整张表。

检索：按 A–H 类或精确 ID 查找。每项提供名称/关键词、摄影类别、可复用句型、适用条件。先明确起止构图、相机路径与主体动作，再决定是否需要幅度、速度和落幅停顿；普通幅度和速度不必重复堆词。视角、构图、光学效果可以与兼容的运镜组合；一个 Shot 内的变化不自动代表切镜。

## A 固定与微动

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-A01 固定机位 / 锁定 / static / locked-off | 机位 | The camera holds a completely static shot with unchanged focal length while {visible action} unfolds within the frame. | 构图内人物和环境可动；若允许焦点转移须单独写，不把焦距与对焦距离混为一谈。 |
| CAM-A02 呼吸感微推 / 几乎察觉不到的靠近 | 位移 | The camera pushes forward very slowly over a short distance, tightening the framing only slightly toward {detail}. | 是相机极小幅推进；与人的呼吸、手持上下晃动分别表达。 |
| CAM-A03 轻微手持 / 呼吸式晃动 / handheld | 承托方式 | A handheld camera maintains {framing} with small, irregular body-driven sway as {action} continues. | 晃动幅度轻、存在休止与修正，不采用规律振荡或默认模糊。 |
| CAM-A04 剧烈晃动 / 被撞 / 震动 | 相机扰动 | A sudden {impact or vibration} jolts the camera through a brief large-amplitude shake before it {settles or continues shaking}. | 写出来源与持续时间；主体是否可辨依叙事需求，不用于所有真实感场景。 |

## B 推拉与揭示

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-B01 推镜头 / 慢推 / push in | 前后位移 | The camera slowly pushes from {starting framing} toward {subject or detail}, ending on {closer framing}. | 身体靠近主体；不同于只改焦距的变焦。 |
| CAM-B02 急推 / 撞击推 / crash push | 快速位移 | The camera rapidly pushes a short distance toward {subject}, tightening from {start} to {end}, then decelerates into a hold. | 急推是速度特征；不代表真的撞上人物，结尾是否急停可选。 |
| CAM-B03 拉镜头 / pull out | 后退位移 | The camera slowly pulls back from {close detail} to {wider framing}, revealing {surroundings}. | 需要退行空间；主体可以变小。 |
| CAM-B04 急拉揭示 / sudden reveal | 快速后退 | The camera rapidly pulls back from {detail} to a wide view, revealing {previously hidden situation}, then settles. | 保持连续空间，不把后退瞬间变成无解释的传送。 |
| CAM-B05 光学变焦推 / zoom in | 焦距变化 | With the camera position fixed, the lens zooms in at {speed}, narrowing the field of view and enlarging {subject} in the frame. | 起点不变；不写成相机前移，不把单纯变焦当成机位改变造成的透视变化。 |
| CAM-B06 希区柯克变焦 / dolly zoom / 眩晕 | 位移加反向变焦 | The camera dollies toward {subject} while zooming out, keeping the subject approximately the same size as the background perspective changes. | 也可后退加变焦推近；明确反向配合与主体尺度，不写主体和背景都完全不变。 |
| CAM-B07 前景遮挡揭示 / 门框滑开 / reveal | 横向位移 | The camera slowly trucks sideways until {foreground obstruction} moves out of the sightline, revealing {subject behind it}. | 需要前后景关系和可行视差；不是穿透遮挡物，也不是原地摇镜的默认替代。 |

## C 摇镜与俯仰

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-C01 水平摇镜 / pan / 横摇 | 原地水平转向 | From a fixed camera position, the camera pans {left or right} at an even speed from {start target} to {end target}. | 相机不横向位移；要出现滑动视差时应选横移。 |
| CAM-C02 甩镜 / 急摇 / whip pan | 快速水平转向 | The camera whip-pans {direction}, producing brief directional motion blur before landing on {new target}. | 起落幅均写清；是连续甩动还是借此隐藏剪辑需确认。 |
| CAM-C03 垂直摇上 / tilt up | 原地向上转向 | From a fixed position, the camera slowly tilts up from {lower detail} to {upper target}. | 相机不整体升高；由脚到头或由地面到天空只是可换端点。 |
| CAM-C04 垂直摇下 / tilt down | 原地向下转向 | From a fixed position, the camera slowly tilts down from {upper view} to {lower subject or detail}. | 与从高处下降的位移区分。 |
| CAM-C05 摇镜落幅定人 / pan and hold | 摇镜加停止 | The camera pans {direction} toward {target}, slows as it arrives, and holds a stable {ending framing}. | 明确停止目标和是否减速；不可同时全程匀速不停。 |

## D 平移与跟随

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-D01 横移 / truck / lateral move | 横向位移 | The camera trucks {left or right} along a path parallel to {subject or scene plane}, maintaining {distance and orientation}. | 若要求距离和角度不变，主体与路径应能支持；背景因视差变化。 |
| CAM-D02 侧面跟拍 / side tracking | 跟随位移 | The camera tracks beside the moving subject at a matched pace, maintaining {screen position and framing}. | 速度匹配主体；不是固定机位让人穿过画面。 |
| CAM-D03 背后跟拍 / follow from behind | 跟随位移 | The camera follows behind {subject} at {distance}, keeping the back and the path ahead within {framing}. | 主体朝前走；要看表情须另设计转头、反射或切镜。 |
| CAM-D04 前置倒退跟拍 / backward tracking | 跟随位移 | Facing the approaching subject, the camera tracks backward at a matched pace, holding {framing} on the face and steps. | 摄影机在人物前方后退；与前摄自拍不同，路径须可行。 |
| CAM-D05 载具跟拍 / vehicle-mounted | 安装关系与位移 | Mounted to {vehicle}, the camera moves with it along {route}, showing {relative subject motion and passing surroundings}. | 区分车内固定机位、拍车外及外部跟车；不默认高速或剧烈颠簸。 |

## E 升降与俯拍

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-E01 升镜头 / pedestal up | 垂直位移 | The entire camera rises slowly from {initial height} to {ending height}, revealing {new spatial information}. | 是否俯仰补偿需明确；单纯升高不必然得到完整全景。 |
| CAM-E02 降镜头 / pedestal down | 垂直位移 | The camera descends from {height and framing} toward {ending subject and height}, settling at {ending view}. | 与原地摇下区分；地面附近减速避免无意穿地。 |
| CAM-E03 摇臂大范围 / crane sweep | 复合位移 | In one broad crane move, the camera rises and sweeps {direction} from {start} to {end}, maintaining attention on {subject}. | 需要可行弧线和空间；写明镜头朝向是否随路径调整。 |
| CAM-E04 天顶俯拍 / overhead / bird's-eye | 视角 | Directly above {subject or layout}, the camera looks vertically downward, presenting {spatial arrangement} within {framing}. | 视角本身不是升降；静止、横移或旋转另选。 |

## F 环绕

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-F01 半环绕 / 半圈 / 180度 / arc | 围主体位移 | The camera arcs approximately half a circle around {subject}, maintaining {distance and height} while facing the subject. | 起止方位和镜轴关系需明确；不默认角色随镜头转身。 |
| CAM-F02 360度环绕 / 绕一圈 / orbit | 围主体位移 | The camera makes one complete orbit around {subject} at {pace}, keeping the subject framed as the surrounding space changes continuously. | 需足够时间及无遮挡环形路径；多面展示增加连续性难度，不保证身份稳定。 |
| CAM-F03 螺旋上升环绕 / rising orbit | 环绕加升高 | The camera circles {subject} while gradually rising from {start height} to {end height}, maintaining {subject framing}. | 同时定义环绕范围和上升幅度；与沿自身光轴翻滚不同。 |
| CAM-F04 低角度贴地环绕 / low orbit | 低位环绕 | At a low height near the ground, the camera arcs around {subject}, looking upward with {arc extent and speed}. | 镜头不穿地，脚部和遮挡关系需保持；低位不是自动仰角翻滚。 |

## G 视角与光学

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-G01 第一人称 / POV / 角色眼睛 | 视角 | From {character}'s eye-level point of view, the camera shows {what they see}, with {head movement if needed}. | 通常不直接看到自己的脸；自拍属于手机视角，不等同眼睛 POV。 |
| CAM-G02 过肩 / OTS / 对话反打 | 构图与视角 | Over {foreground character}'s shoulder, the camera frames {opposite subject}, with the shoulder occupying {side of frame}. | 交代前后角色与视线，保持镜轴；无需强制相机运动。 |
| CAM-G03 FPV穿越 / 穿越机 / fly-through | 高速路径 | In an FPV-style move, the camera flies through {open passage} and emerges into {larger space}, following {continuous route}. | 是通过真实开口，不穿实体；速度和转向按空间可行性调整。 |
| CAM-G04 航拍 / drone / aerial | 高位平台与视角 | From an aerial viewpoint, the camera moves {path and speed} above {landscape}, keeping {target} within the composition. | 高空俯瞰不自动等于下降；按用户选择高度、方向及构图。 |
| CAM-G05 微距 / macro / 极小细节 | 光学与景别 | A macro view reveals {small subject}, with the focal plane on {detail} and {background rendering appropriate to the setup}. | 不默认全物体都锐利或永远极浅景深；物体尺度、对焦与机位稳定性重要。 |
| CAM-G06 镜面反射 / 倒影 / reflection | 观察路径 | The camera frames {subject}'s reflection in {mirror, water or glass}, maintaining coherent reflected geometry as {action occurs}. | 倒影动作、视线和遮挡需匹配；不能无故多出人物，反射不是必然转场。 |

## H 风格化与特殊

| ID / 名称与关键词 | 类别 | 可复用英文句型 | 适用条件与边界 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAM-H01 荷兰角 / 倾斜地平线 / Dutch angle | 构图 | The shot holds a {small or pronounced} Dutch angle, with the horizon tilted {direction}. | 倾斜的静态构图；不意味着相机持续翻滚。 |
| CAM-H02 镜头翻滚 / roll / 光轴旋转 | 旋转 | The camera rolls {clockwise or counterclockwise} around its optical axis through {extent}, ending at {orientation}. | 不绕主体走圆，也不必改变机位或景别。 |
| CAM-H03 子弹时间 / bullet time | 时间效果与环绕 | The subject's {action} unfolds in pronounced slow motion while the viewpoint arcs around it, revealing {detail of the action}. | 风格化效果，不能同时要求所有动作均为实时纪实；路径、时间尺度需说明。 |
| CAM-H04 焦点转移 / 拉焦 / rack focus | 对焦 | With the camera position fixed, focus shifts from the sharp foreground {A} to the previously soft background {B}, leaving {A} out of focus. | 可反向；两对象位置不交换。与焦距变焦、相机推近和对焦搜索区分。 |
| CAM-H05 一镜到底 / long take / 无切镜 | 剪辑约束与连续路径 | In one uninterrupted take, the camera follows {subject} through {connected spaces}, transitioning from {start framing} to {end framing} without cuts. | 给出连续可行路线；内部动作可分阶段但不能新增 cuts。 |
| CAM-H06 运动模糊慢门 / 拖尾 / slow shutter look | 成像效果 | During {movement}, pronounced directional motion blur trails the moving forms, while {stable reference} remains readable where appropriate. | 由相对运动决定模糊；不同时要求运动边缘全程极锐，也不保证真实曝光参数模拟。 |
| CAM-H07 抽离式拉远 / 主体被环境吞没 | 拉远的叙事功能 | The camera continuously retreats into a much wider view, allowing {subject} to shrink until the surrounding {environment} dominates the frame. | 与 B03 同类但强调最终尺度；不能同时主体大小始终不变。 |
| CAM-H08 穿物转场 / 水面、镜面、屏幕 | 风格化转场 | The camera approaches {surface or opening}; as it fills the frame, the view transitions into {destination space}, preserving {visual bridge}. | 洞口可真实穿行；实体镜面/屏幕属于特效转场，标明而不宣称符合纯纪实物理。 |
| CAM-H09 电梯视角 / 垂直移动狭窄空间 | 安装关系或位移 | From {inside or outside the elevator}, the camera {remains fixed to the cabin or tracks its vertical movement}, showing {relative motion}. | 必须选择关系；随轿厢固定时内部不必产生整体上下滑动的背景。 |
| CAM-H10 门缝窥视 / 窗框限制 / peeking | 遮挡构图 | Through {narrow doorway or gap}, the camera frames {subject}, with the foreground edges restricting the view. | 不默认偷窥叙事；遮挡范围与可见内容需兼容，不强行拍到被遮住的区域。 |
| CAM-H11 摇臂俯冲 / 快速下降 | 高位快速位移 | The camera rapidly descends from {high viewpoint} toward {subject}, then slows into {ending framing}. | 需落幅和减速；与 E02 相似，按速度和戏剧功能选择，不叠为两次下降。 |
| CAM-H12 推近锁定 / 急推后定住 | 位移与停止 | The camera pushes rapidly toward {subject}, then comes to a firm stop at {ending framing} and holds. | 与 B02 可合并检索；“定住”是明确末态，不必额外添加震动。 |
| CAM-H13 螺旋推进 / 钻入 | 位移加光轴旋转 | The camera pushes forward while rolling {direction} around its optical axis, creating a spiral approach toward {target}. | 与 F03 区分：这里是前进加翻滚，而不是围着主体边绕边升。 |
